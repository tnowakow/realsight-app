## Mission: Create 5-7 realistic acquisition target scenarios to serve as demo data for the new "Acquisitions" feature.

This data will be used by Marcus (Backend) to build the scoring engine and Maya (Frontend) to build the UI. Each scenario should include enough detail to justify its final acquisition score.

### Target 1: "Value-Add Retail Strip"

- **Property Name:** Sunset Plaza
- **Address:** 123 Main Street, Phoenix, AZ
- **Property Type:** Retail Strip Center
- **Deal Type:** Value-Add
- **Narrative:** An aging but well-located retail strip with rents ~20% below market rate. High traffic count, but deferred maintenance has led to current vacancies. Requires capital injection for facade renovation and tenant improvements to attract higher-paying national tenants.
- **Key Data:**
    - **Purchase Price:** $4,500,000
    - **Square Footage:** 25,000 sqft
    - **Current Occupancy:** 75%
    - **Current NOI:** $270,000
    - **Market Cap Rate:** 6.5%
    - **Pro-forma NOI (Post-Renovation):** $425,000
    - **Renovation Budget:** $750,000
    - **Lease Rollover:** 3 of 5 remaining tenants have leases expiring in the next 18 months.
- **Scoring Justification:**
    - **Financials:** Low initial return, but very high NOI growth potential.
    - **Value-Add:** Classic value-add play with clear upside from below-market rents and renovation.
    - **Market/Location:** Excellent. High-growth submarket.
    - **Property:** Physical condition is fair; requires investment. Tenant quality is currently low.

### Target 2: "Distressed Office Building"

- **Property Name:** Metro Business Tower
- **Address:** 456 Commerce Drive, Detroit, MI
- **Property Type:** Office (Class B)
- **Deal Type:** Distressed
- **Narrative:** The primary tenant, a law firm occupying 40% of the building, went bankrupt and vacated, triggering co-tenancy clauses for two other tenants. The out-of-state owner is unmotivated and facing foreclosure, creating a significant price discount opportunity.
- **Key Data:**
    - **Purchase Price:** $8,000,000 (Market value was $15M)
    - **Square Footage:** 120,000 sqft
    - **Current Occupancy:** 35%
    - **Current NOI:** $300,000 (was $1,100,000)
    - **Market Cap Rate:** 8.0%
    - **Pro-forma NOI (Post-Lease-Up):** $1,200,000
    - **Lease-Up Time Estimate:** 24-36 months
    - **Tenant Improvement Budget:** $1,500,000
- **Scoring Justification:**
    - **Financials:** High risk, negative cash flow initially, but massive upside on purchase price.
    - **Value-Add:** Primarily a lease-up and stabilization play.
    - **Market/Location:** Stable but not high-growth. Dependent on attracting new major tenant.
    - **Property:** Good physical condition, just vacant.

### Target 3: "Stabilized Industrial Warehouse"

- **Property Name:** Apex Logistics Center
- **Address:** 789 Distribution Way, Indianapolis, IN
- **Property Type:** Industrial / Warehouse
- **Deal Type:** Stabilized / Core
- **Narrative:** Fully-leased industrial property with a credit-worthy national logistics tenant (e.g., FedEx Supply Chain) on a long-term NNN (triple-net) lease. Low management intensity, predictable cash flow. Ideal for a conservative, income-focused investor.
- **Key Data:**
    - **Purchase Price:** $12,000,000
    - **Square Footage:** 150,000 sqft
    - **Current Occupancy:** 100%
    - **Current NOI:** $780,000
    - **Market Cap Rate:** 6.5%
    - **Lease Term Remaining:** 8.5 years
    - **Rent Escalations:** 2.5% annually
- **Scoring Justification:**
    - **Financials:** Strong, stable, predictable returns. Low growth potential.
    - **Value-Add:** Minimal. This is a cash-flow asset, not a growth one.
    - **Market/Location:** Prime logistics corridor.
    - **Property:** Excellent condition. High-quality tenant. High concentration risk (single tenant).

### Target 4: "Emerging Market Multifamily"

- **Property Name:** The Residences at Oakwood
- **Address:** 321 Growth Avenue, Austin, TX
- **Property Type:** Multifamily Apartment Complex
- **Deal Type:** Growth Market
- **Narrative:** A well-maintained apartment complex in a rapidly growing tech hub. Rents are at market rate, but the submarket is projected to see 5-7% annual rent growth for the next 3-5 years due to corporate relocations and population influx.
- **Key Data:**
    - **Purchase Price:** $25,000,000
    - **Number of Units:** 150
    - **Current Occupancy:** 98%
    - **Current NOI:** $1,250,000
    - **Market Cap Rate:** 5.0%
    - **Projected Rent Growth (Year 1):** 6%
- **Scoring Justification:**
    - **Financials:** Tight initial cap rate, but strong projected income growth.
    - **Value-Add:** No physical value-add; this is a market-timing play.
    - **Market/Location:** Exceptional. The entire thesis rests on the market's growth.
    - **Property:** Good condition, easy to manage.

### Target 5: "Mixed-Use Repositioning"

- **Property Name:** The Foundry Lofts
- **Address:** 654 Factory Row, Pittsburgh, PA
- **Property Type:** Mixed-Use (Retail on ground floor, old office above)
- **Deal Type:** Repositioning / Heavy Value-Add
- **Narrative:** A historic building with stable ground-floor retail but nearly vacant, outdated office space on the upper three floors. The city recently passed zoning changes allowing for residential conversion. The play is to convert the office floors to high-end residential lofts.
- **Key Data:**
    - **Purchase Price:** $6,000,000
    - **Total Square Footage:** 80,000 sqft (20k retail, 60k office)
    - **Current Occupancy:** 25% (Retail only)
    - **Current NOI:** $150,000
    - **Conversion Budget:** $10,000,000
    - **Pro-forma NOI (Post-Conversion):** $1,300,000
    - **Construction Time:** 18 months
- **Scoring Justification:**
    - **Financials:** Very high risk and capital intensive. Highest potential return if executed correctly.
    - **Value-Add:** Total repositioning of the asset.
    - **Market/Location:** Strong demand for urban loft-style apartments.
    - **Property:** Requires significant construction expertise.
